# Version 0.1 Drafting Notes — October 29, 2026

These notes record how the concept sections in `content/02` through
`content/09` were first drafted, so readers can see where the text came from
and which choices were made along the way. The draft was prepared with AI
assistance. Version 0.1 is meant to be basically right rather than complete; it
has not had a detailed review, and imperfections are expected.

## Basis

The September 15, 2026 Confluence concept page is the main source. Its sections
were reorganized into the core document with light editing. Material from the
February 2026 Proposal, by way of the October 2026 Primer, was added where the
concept page was silent and the material is consistent with it.

## Choices made while drafting

1. **Three pillars became "a third input."** The Primer calls Contextual
   Conditions a third pillar of interoperability. The concept page does not use
   that framing. The table was kept because it is a clear way in, but retitled
   as inputs to CDS to match the concept page's evaluation-scoped view.
2. **Definition follows the concept page.** The Primer's "standardized, coded
   assertion" was replaced by the concept page's two terms: Contextual
   Condition and Contextual Condition assertion (finding 5 of the initial
   review).
3. **Nullification was left out.** The Primer's fourth value state concerns
   stored assertions, which WD-7 places outside this document (OQ-5).
4. **Workflow material was left out.** VXU and QBP behavior, OBX-14 rules,
   storage, and correction are referred to topics 140 and 141 under WD-7. Only
   the date rule (WD-8) was kept, and stated for every representation.
5. **Pitfalls were adjusted.** The Primer's "persisting QBP context" pitfall
   was dropped as workflow. "Using OBX-14 as onset or expiration" became the
   general "carrying a meaningful date in a timestamp field." "Making the CDS
   engine infer the condition" and the others were kept, using Schedule
   Authority and Knowledge Base in place of "CDS authority."
6. **Numeric datatype left open.** The concept page says integer; the draft
   French inventory includes decimal-looking Number values. Recorded as OQ-6
   with a CONFIRM note in section 03.
7. **Role vocabulary follows the concept page.** Section 06 uses Schedule
   Authority, Knowledge Base, and terminology steward. The four-role model from
   the Proposal is not repeated; OQ-4 remains open, with a CONFIRM note.
8. **Representation principles are partly new wording.** "Sparse," "absence is
   unknown," and "vocabulary, not structure" restate points already in the
   sources. "Support is reported, not assumed" restates the concept page's
   Incremental Support section. Whether every representation must report
   recognition per condition is for topics 1 and 137 to settle.
9. **FHIR is described thinly.** The FHIR paragraph says only that HALO factors
   are Contextual Conditions supplied to the ImmDS + HALO interface (WD-6), and
   leaves the representation to topic 1.
